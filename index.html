Controls

PC: WASD move, mouse aim (click to lock), Left click fire/place, Right click aim, R reload, Space jump, Shift sprint, 1/2 guns, 3 pickaxe, V melee (3x from behind), G throw grenade, T switch frag/plasma, Z wall, X floor, C ramp, Q build on/off, E edit, F enter/exit vehicle (helicopter: Space up, Shift down; jet: W/S throttle, mouse steers; in a vehicle: click = main gun, right click = rockets/cannon, G = mech stomp), M overview map, Tab scores, Esc pause.
Phone: left thumb moves, drag right side to look, FIRE (drag to aim while firing), AIM, JUMP, R, WPN cycles guns/pickaxe, MELEE, NADE throws, TYPE switches frag/plasma, ENTER also swaps for a gun on the ground, BUILD cycles wall/floor/ramp/off, EDIT, ENTER gets in/out of a vehicle (aircraft: JUMP climbs, push stick far to descend) (AIM fires rockets/cannon, EDIT stomps).
